Our home is your home

A little piece of Lahore,
on London Road.

Step through the door and the mud-washed walls, pressed-tin ceilings and mandala coasters take you somewhere else. Chaachi's is a family-run café where everything is made fresh by Chaachi herself, from the kunafa cake and tiramisu to the pasties and the dish of the day.

The walls tell the story of the neighbourhood through generations of photographs taken in Norbury. Come for a karak chai, stay for the conversation.

Our story

From soft opening to South London's new favourite

  1. 2025

    The doors open

    A soft opening weekend where guests "practically met the whole family". Pomegranate tea from Turkey, Spanish lattes and shepherd's pie win hearts from day one.

  2. Summer 2026

    Back & better

    After a short break Chaachi's reopens to a queue of regulars, with peach matcha, iced Spanish latte with cold foam and new hot dishes of the day.

  3. Sept 2026

    New desserts & flavours

    Blackforest and Oreo cheesecakes join the counter, alongside a Birthday Cake Matcha and Biscoff Latte for team matcha and team coffee.

  4. 5 Oct 2026

    Open earlier, every day

    Doors now open at 3.30pm daily, Wednesdays included, giving you an extra day of Chaachi's every week.

  5. 15 Nov 2026

    Chaachi's Events begin

    The first Sip & Paint sells out and raises money for a lovely cause. South London has a new event space, and the next get-together is already being planned.
